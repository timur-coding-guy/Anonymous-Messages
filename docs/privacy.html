Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate the service:

  • Hashed user ID: Your Telegram user ID is hashed using SHA-256 with an additional salt before storage
  • Language Preference: Your selected language (two-letter code: en, ru, ua)
  • Browser Preferences: The website stores your selected language and storage choice in your browser after you accept

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Important: We do NOT store or log:

  • Message content
  • Message metadata
  • Usernames or real names
  • IP addresses
  • Any identifying information beyond hashed user IDs

3. Data Storage and Security

We protect stored data as follows:

  • User IDs are hashed using SHA-256 with salt before storage
  • Data is stored in a Turso database
  • No message content is ever stored in our database

4. Data Retention

We retain only the minimum data necessary for service operation:

  • Hashed user IDs and language preferences are retained until you request deletion
  • No message content is retained

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties. Messages are transmitted directly through Telegram's infrastructure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your data from our database
  • Change your language preference at any time
  • Stop using the service at any time

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in servers located outside your country. By using the service, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will be available on this page.
