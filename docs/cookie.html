Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Browser Storage

This website does not use cookies or access your location. When no language preference is saved, it selects a language locally from your browser settings. Your language choice is saved in local storage only after you accept.

2. Browser Storage We Use

The following items are stored in your browser's local storage:

Storage Item Storage Type Purpose Retention
language Local storage Stores your selected language after you accept Until cleared in your browser
cookieConsent Local storage Stores your choice to accept or reject language storage Until cleared in your browser

3. Third-Party Tracking

We do not use third-party storage, analytics, marketing, or tracking scripts on this website.

4. Your Choice

When you first visit the website, a banner lets you choose whether to save your language preference between visits:

  • Accept: Your selected language is saved in local storage and restored on future visits
  • Reject: Your language is not saved; it is selected again from your browser settings on your next visit

5. Managing Browser Storage

You can clear this website's local storage through your browser settings. This will remove your saved language and choice, and the banner will appear again on your next visit.

  • View the data stored by this website
  • Clear this website's stored data

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
